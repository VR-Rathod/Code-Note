# **History**
	- **How**: Wix was founded in **2006** by **Avishai Abrahami**, **Nadav Abrahami**, and **Gidi Torbey** as an easy-to-use website builder designed to help individuals and businesses create websites without coding skills.
	- **Who**: Wix was developed by **Wix.com Ltd.**, a company based in Tel Aviv, Israel, with a goal of providing a drag-and-drop website builder accessible to users of all technical levels.
	- **Why**: Wix was created to make website building accessible to everyone, offering an intuitive platform for users to design professional websites without needing any technical expertise.
- # Introduction
	- ## Advantages:
		- **Ease of Use**: Wix’s drag-and-drop editor is highly intuitive, making it accessible to beginners and non-technical users who want to build their own websites quickly.
		- **Customizable Templates**: Wix offers a wide variety of pre-designed templates that can be customized to fit different industries and purposes.
		- **App Market**: Wix has an extensive app marketplace, allowing users to add additional features, such as e-commerce functionality, booking systems, and social media integrations.
		- **Responsive Design**: Websites built on Wix are mobile-friendly, with automatic adjustments made to ensure good performance across devices.
		- **Free Plan Available**: Wix offers a free plan with basic features, making it accessible for personal use or small projects with no upfront cost.
		- **Built-in SEO Tools**: Wix provides tools for optimizing your website for search engines, helping users improve their website’s visibility and ranking.
	-
	- ## Disadvantages:
		- **Limited Customization**: Although Wix offers great design flexibility, it is still somewhat limited in terms of advanced customizations when compared to open-source platforms like **WordPress**.
		- **Template Lock-In**: Once you select a template and build your site, you cannot easily change it without rebuilding your content, which can be inconvenient if you want to switch designs later.
		- **Performance Issues**: Some users report that Wix websites can be slower to load, especially as more features are added or if the site becomes more complex.
		- **Limited Control Over Hosting**: Wix handles all hosting aspects, which means users have less control over their hosting environment compared to self-hosted solutions.
		- **Free Plan Limitations**: While Wix offers a free plan, it comes with ads, limited storage, and no custom domain, which may not be suitable for businesses or professional sites.
		-
	- # Remember Points:
		- **Beginner-Friendly**: Wix is designed for people with no coding experience, providing an intuitive drag-and-drop interface and a variety of customizable templates.
		- **Great for Personal Websites and Small Businesses**: Ideal for users who need a simple website for personal use, portfolios, or small business sites, with options for online stores and booking systems.
		- **Limited Customization**: While Wix is flexible, it’s not as customizable as more complex platforms like **WordPress** or **Squarespace**, especially for advanced features or large-scale websites.
		- **Free and Paid Plans**: Offers both free and premium plans, with the premium plans providing access to more features, such as custom domains, e-commerce tools, and removing Wix ads.
		- **SEO and Mobile-Friendly**: Wix includes built-in SEO tools and ensures that all websites are responsive and mobile-optimized.
